**YourWorldTime sets no cookies.** Not analytics cookies, not preference cookies, not "strictly necessary" cookies. There is no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to.

## What is stored on your device

Three values in **local storage**, which is not a cookie: it is never sent with a request, so no server — including ours — can read it.

**YourWorldTime-theme** : "light" or "dark", if you used the theme toggle. Persists until you clear site data.

**YourWorldTime-planner-zones** : The list of time zones you last had in the meeting planner.

**YourWorldTime-pinned-cities** : The cities you pinned on the world clock.

Everything else — the zones in a conversion, the target of a countdown — lives in the URL, which is why sharing a link shares the state.

## Third-party cookies

None. The site loads no third-party scripts, no embedded content, no fonts from a font CDN and no images from another origin. Its Content Security Policy blocks connections to any host other than its own, so a third-party cookie cannot be set even by accident.

## If advertising is enabled later

The site reserves space for advertising but serves none. If that changes, this page will be updated before it does, and:

- The advertising provider would set its own cookies, which are used to measure and, if permitted, personalise advertising.
- Visitors in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland would see a consent request from a certified consent management platform _before_ any advertising cookie is set, with a refusal as easy to give as an acceptance.
- A "manage cookies" control would be added to the footer so a choice can be changed at any time.

## Controlling storage

Clearing site data for this domain in your browser removes the three local storage values. The site works normally afterwards; it will use your system's light or dark preference and forget your pinned cities.
